I'm Beryl, a freelance writer, content creator, and aspiring digital strategist with a passion for turning ideas into clear, engaging, and purposeful content.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Commerce in Business Administration, giving me a strong foundation in business, marketing, communication, and strategic thinking. I also hold an ICDL Certificate and have completed a Fundamentals of Digital Marketing course.

My work combines creativity with research and attention to detail. Whether I'm writing a blog post, creating social media content, developing SEO-focused copy, or working on a website, my goal is always the same: create work that communicates clearly and delivers results.

Beyond writing, I have an interest in web design, digital marketing, branding, and technology. I enjoy learning new tools and finding better ways to help individuals and businesses build a stronger digital presence.
